C. Procedures
     The participants were grouped randomly and three
evaluation sessions were held separately for each group.
During each session a briefing was given before the
participants began their evaluation exercise. The eighteen
prototypes were navigated by each participant in a systematic
and controlled manner. The navigation involved using a                            Figure 2. Mean rating for arouse/high dimension from each of the prototype.
