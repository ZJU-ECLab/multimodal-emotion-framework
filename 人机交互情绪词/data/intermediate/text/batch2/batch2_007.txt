physiological responses to recognize human emotions
according to the constructed emotion taxonomy. The reason
behind this selection is that although detecting human
emotions based on facial, behavioral, text and speech are

gestures or behaviors can be proven to be not very effective
at times since these can be consciously altered. Due to this
reason, the researchers have diverted their focus to studying
