switching between different difficulty levels was based on
performance, excellent performance resulted in increase in the

highest level), good performance caused the level to remain
constant at the current level, and poor performance resulted in
decrease in difficulty level (except when the player was
already at the lowest level). Similarly, it can be seen that when
the switching between different difficulty levels was based on
anxiety, low anxiety resulted in increase in the level of

the current level, and high anxiety resulted in decrease in

                                                                                           On arrival a participant was taken to the experiment room
                                                                                      where he/she would be seated in front of the robot where the
                                                                                      tasks were presented. During the first session, the participants
                                                                                      were told about the sensors - their purpose, the method of
                                                                                      attaching sensors (non-invasive) and their safety. After the
                                                                                      sensors were placed, the participants were given some leisure
                                                                                      reading material and asked to relax for 5 minutes while their
                                                                                      baseline reading was taken. Then, the experimenter started the
                                                                                      session after briefing the participant regarding the rules of the
                                                                                      task and general procedure. Once the session started, the
                                                                                      participant was not disturbed for the rest of the session. After
                                                                                      every epoch, the participant reported regarding his/her
                                                                                      assessment of one's own emotion on a nine point Likert scale.
                                                                                      There were a total of 5 questions, each of which inquired
                                                                                      about the level engagement, anxiety, anger, frustration and
                                                                                      challenge. At the end of each task, the participant answered
                                                                                      questions pertaining to their overall experience during the
                                                                                      entire session. This included their overall engagement,
                                                                                      challenge and self-evaluation of their performance.
                                                                                                    V. RESULTS AND DISCUSSION
                                                                                           The results presented here are based on the validation
                                                                                      sessions RBB, and RBB2.
                                                                                           Fig. 6 shows the average subjective anxiety of the
                                                                                      participants (as reported by them) during the two sessions. It
                                                                                      can be seen that out of 14 participants, 11 reported decrease in
                                                                                      anxiety, 1 reported an increase (P13) and 2 reported no change
                                                                                      in anxiety (P9 and P14) during the anxiety-based task as
                                                                                      compared to the performance-based task. This was a
                                                                                      significant result as the anxiety-based sessions utilized the
                                                                                      predictions regarding the probable anxiety level of the
                                                                                      participant to continuously adapt the task difficulty to keep the
                                                                                      participant in a lower anxiety state. Using Chi-square test, it
                                                                                      was observed that the null hypothesis (asserting that there was
                                                                                      no change in anxiety between performance-based and anxiety-
                                                                                      based sessions) could be rejected at 99.99% confidence
                                                                                      interval.
                                                                                           The detected anxiety level was calculated based on the
                                                                                      physiological indices and regression tree model for each
                                                                                      epoch per participant. The average accuracy across all the 14
                                                                                      participants was approximately 70%. Note that there are two
                                                                                      factors that contribute toward the predictive accuracy. First, if
                                                                                      the physiological features for anxiety are not prominent for a
                                                                                      particular participant then the model will not be very accurate.
