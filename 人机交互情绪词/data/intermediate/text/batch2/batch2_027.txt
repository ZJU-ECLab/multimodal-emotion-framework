    Abstract— This paper introduces Pepe Jr, a 4 DOF robotic
lamp, designed as a cost-effective platform for Human Robot
Interaction research. We discuss the mechanical and electronic
design aspects of the robot, and the design of the motion patterns
associated with each emotion expressed by the robot. We also
discuss results from a battery of surveys to gauge user perception
of these expressed emotions, which validate the design objectives
of the robot.

  Keywords—Sociable robots, Robotics, Robotic Technology,
Human robot interaction, Robotic desk Lamp, Emotion; Expression
