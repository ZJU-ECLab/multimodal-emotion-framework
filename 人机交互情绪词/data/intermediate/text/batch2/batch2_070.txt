                                                                              B. Texture Mapping
                                                                                 In order to generate a good looking virtual face, the
                                                                              cylindrical projection method [23] was adopted to map the 2D
                                                                              image texture to the 3-D face model. The 2D image applied
                                                                              for texture mapping has been processed and beautified. In this
                                                                              case, when the processed 2D texture image was mapped onto
                                                                              the 3-D virtual talking head, the 3-D virtual talking head was
                                                                              particularly realistic as a whole. And in the process of 3-D
                                                                              virtual talking head animation, the texture was dynamically
                                                                              adjusted as the model changes. The 3-D virtual talking head
                                                                              after texture mapping has the advantage of easy acceptance
                                                                              and high affinity in the process of human-computer
                                                                              interaction. When it was used as an object of
                                                                              human-computer interaction, it could enhance the
                                                                              communication between the real user and the virtual person,
                                                                              and even improve the user experience. Therefore, this texture
                                                                              image not only had a nice appearance but also had a high
                                                                              practical value.
