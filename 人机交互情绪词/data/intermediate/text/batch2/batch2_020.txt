1   INTRODUCTION
Psychological disorders mainly include depression and anxiety, which affect daily work and life
and bring a heavy burden to family and society [1]. According to the study, it is estimated that
more than 350 million people worldwide suffer from depression, and the trend is still rising [2, 3].
   Generalized anxiety disorder (GAD) is a typical anxiety disorder with persistent excessive
and inappropriate worries that are not limited to specific situations, and its common symptoms
are fatigue and difficulty in concentrating [4, 5].
   From a social point of view, sudden disaster events, such as the earthquake and the worldwide
prevalence of coronavirus, will significantly increase the risk of depression, anxiety, and other
psychological abnormalities. Therefore, to reduce the incidence of depression and prevent people
at risk of depression and anxiety from turning into having depression and anxiety, timely screening
and assessment of the level of depression and anxiety and early psychological intervention are
essential.
   The mental state assessment of depression and anxiety can be determined by the scores obtained
by self-assessment of the self-rating scale (BDI-2 [6] or PHQ-8/9 [7, 8] for depression, Generalized
Anxiety Disorder-7 (GAD-7) for anxiety [9]). They often rely on self-reports as implicit indica-
tors of mental states, which suffer from recall bias and add to the participant’s cognitive burden.
Therefore, the automatic assessment method of depression and anxiety levels based on human
computer interaction and deep learning is expected to provide objective and rapid assessment,
which is beneficial for depression and anxiety screening and early prevention and control.
   Depression and anxiety recognition based on deep learning can be modeled as classification and
regression problems. At present, a large number of studies have been conducted on the classifica-
tion and assessment of depression. However, anxiety recognition is mainly based on classification
methods, and there is a lack of assessment methods based on regression in deep learning.
   Human computer interaction (HCI) refers to the process of information exchange between
people and computers in a certain way to complete certain tasks. It is widely used such as in
industrial design, sports physiology, physiological psychology, psychological and behavioral sci-
ence, and so on [10]. Virtual reality (VR), as one of the important practical ways of HCI, provides
participants with rich interaction and a strong sense of environmental immersion by simulating
the real environment [11]. In order to provide the participants with vivid and more immersive
emotional stimulation, this article is based on the HCI technology of VR for the first time, which
induces the participants’ emotional experience more deeply through the three virtual reality scenes

    (1) Pupil-wave signal collected by HCI based on VR emotional scenes is developed to assess
        the level of depression for the first time. In previous studies, the most frequently used
        signals for depression level assessment are behavioral signals induced by traditional in-
        teraction methods such as self-statements, music, emotional expression, autobiographical
        recall, and films [12, 13]; these signals mainly include video and audio signals [14–23].
        The traditional interaction methods can’t provide the participants with a strong emotional
        stimulation experience with a strong sense of immersion, and the behavioral signals are
        the indirect expression of emotion, which is easy to camouflage and disguise, so it is not
        enough to use these behavioral signals to assess emotional state [24]. As a physiological
        index, pupil diameter change is closely related to emotion [25, 26]. Compared with be-
        havioral signals such as facial expression, speech, and language, pupil-wave can directly
        reflect the most instinctive and undisguised emotional expressions. Therefore, it can be
        used as an objective signal to assess mental state. In particular, compared with emotion-
        inducing methods based on traditional interaction methods, HCI based on VR technology
        can be used to simulate the real emotional environment, allowing participants to partici-
        pate, and helping to produce a continuous and more real emotional experience [27, 28].
    (2) The deep learning regression method is used to assess the level of anxiety for the first
        time. In previous studies, the identification of anxiety is mainly based on the classification
        method [29–31], and there is a lack of research on anxiety level assessment based on the
        regression method. Depression and generalized anxiety have common emotional expres-
        sions [32]. Depression is often accompanied by anxiety, and there is a high correlation
        between them.
    (3) A deep convolution neural network suitable for one-dimensional signal processing is con-
        structed. First, we improve the multi-scale convolution neural network architecture and
        introduce more multi-scale features into the model through multiple cascade parallel con-
        volution kernels with different sizes. In the task of depression and anxiety level assess-
        ment, there are significant differences in emotional accumulation time, reaction intensity,
        and duration among participants in different states of depression and anxiety when re-
        ceiving emotional stimulation. Therefore, capturing pupil-wave features from different
        scales will help learn more multi-scale features related to depression or anxiety and help
        the efficiency of mental state assessment.

   Meanwhile, based on self-attention, we propose a width-channel attention module, which cal-
culates the similarity between feature points in the two dimensions of width and channel. Giving
higher weight to areas with more substantial similarities and more apparent features can improve
feature learning because the ruminant thinking unique to the state of depression and anxiety will
lead to similar emotional representations in the process of emotional experience [33, 34]. At the
same time, time-series signals generally have the problem of information redundancy [35]. There-
fore, the regions with richer representation of depression and anxiety states and the feature points
with stronger similarity can be given a higher weight, so the performance of the model in depres-
sion and anxiety level assessment will be effectively improved.
   Finally, considering that people at risk of depression or anxiety have processing obstacles in
both positive and negative emotional attention, a dual-channel deep network structure for joyful
and sad differential pupil-waves is constructed by using a multi-scale residual convolution module

2 RELATED WORK
2.1 Assessment of Depression Level
Based on behavioral signals such as facial expression, speech, and language, depression assess-
ment methods using traditional machine learning (such as SVM) and deep convolution neural
network (DCNN) have been extensively studied [36].
   The AVEC 2013/2014 [37, 38] and AVEC 2016/2017 [39, 40] depression recognition challenge
based on video and audio datasets greatly promoted the study of depression recognition methods.
Moreover, with the development of deep learning, the research of regression models based on facial
expression and audio using a deep learning model has achieved encouraging results [41–45].
   However, most of the signals used in these assessment methods are behavioral signals, mainly
facial expressions, speech, and language (transcribed text). Although eye images [18, 43] are used
in depression assessment, there is a lack of research on the use of physiological signals that are
more closely related to emotion, which are pupil diameter changes, to assess mental state, including
depression and anxiety. Studies have shown that the change of pupil diameter reflects the change
of emotion [25, 26] and has a significant difference between samples with depression and ordinary
people under external emotional stimulation [46, 47].
   At present, the video and audio signals collected in the mental state assessment are collected
through interviews. Interviews use social situations to arouse specific emotions by communicating
with participants or asking participants to describe personal events with emotional color [48].
Interviews can be conducted by human or virtual characters or through the guidance of a computer
screen [49]. This kind of interview is conducted in a state close to natural communication, and the
emotional performance of the participants’ voice, language, and expression is closer to the daily
performance, so there are two problems: First, compared with the emotional stimulation based
on the film, the interview takes longer [50]. Second, there is no obvious abnormality in the daily
performance of people with mild and mild mental disorders, which leads to a significant decrease
in recognition accuracy (e.g., expression-based: mild depression and moderately severe depression
are 65.71% and 84.48%, with an average of 72.59%; based on voice: mild depression and moderately
severe depression are 8.57% and 70.69%, with an average of 44.44%; head movement: mild and
moderately severe 54.29% and 79.31%, with an average of 65.25%) [51].
   Studies have shown that the emotional disorders of patients with depression are characterized
by insufficient experience of joyful emotions and excessive experience of sadness or restlessness,
which can be stimulated by external emotions, especially the virtual reality technology that sim-
ulates the natural emotional environment and can enable participants to produce continuous and
vivid emotional experiences during the process of human computer interaction, which can im-
prove the performance of mental state assessment.
   Many studies have shown that emotional attention bias occurs in emotionally impaired people,
which is characterized by reduced attention to positive emotional information and excessive atten-
tion to negative emotional information, that is, negative attention bias. With the direct expression

the most instinctive emotional changes.

2.2 Assessment of Anxiety Level
Anxiety disorder is one of the most common mental disorders, which mainly includes GAD, So-
cial Anxiety Disorder (SAD), Posttraumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD), Panic Disorder (PD),
and agoraphobia. GAD is characterized by excessive and inappropriate worry, which is persistent
and not limited to specific situations [5]. Some common symptoms are fatigue and difficulty in
concentration. SAD is a chronic and crippling disorder associated with low quality of life, severe
malfunction (serious malfunction), and psychiatric comorbidities [52]. PTSD is a common anxi-
ety disorder and is often associated with multiple traumas, which usually lasts for many years.
Anxiety disorder seriously affects quality of life and is closely related to individual emotional fac-
tors, which are highly complex. Anxiety, especially anxiety levels, should be assessed, for early
psychological intervention and treatment to prevent further anxiety disorder or depression.
   At present, the assessment method of anxiety based on machine learning is the main classi-
fied method, and the research on the classification of anxiety is based on the traditional machine
learning method [29–31]. There is even less research on the assessment of anxiety levels. The as-
sessment of the anxiety level of GAD still depends on the GAD-7 scale (0–21 points: 0–4 minimal,
5–9 mild, 10–14 moderate, 15–21 severe) [9].

2.3 Assessment Method
With the continuous breakthrough of deep learning technology, more and more depression as-
sessment studies use deep learning methods [15–18]. The pupil-wave data used in this article is
one-dimensional time-series data collected by human computer interaction based on virtual reality
emotional scenes. The deep learning model for one-dimensional time series data includes a cyclic
neural network (including RNN, LSTM, etc.) and a one-dimensional convolution neural network
(1D-CNN). Among them, RNN (cyclic neural network) is recursive in the evolution direction of the
sequence, and a chain connects all recurrent nodes. It can capture the correlation of information
before and after the time-series signal, so it is often used in natural language processing. 1D-CNN
has the distinct advantages of convolution neural network-local connection and weight sharing,
so it is better at extracting pattern features. At the same time, 1D-CNN also has the advantages
of low computational overhead, lightweight, and strong fitting ability [53]. In this study, we hope
that the deep learning model can extract the pupil-wave patterns of participants during emotional
stimulation. At the same time, the lightweight feature of 1D-CNN will allow us to build a model
with a more robust representation under the condition of limited computing resources. Therefore,
there is no doubt that 1D-CNN is more suitable for the backbone structure of this study.
   An effective CNN model structure will significantly improve the performance of the model.
In recent years, as an essential idea of CNN modeling, the multi-scale structure has been widely
studied, and state-of-art performance has been obtained in many fields [54–58]. The multi-scale
method requires the model to extract features from multiple receptive fields to describe objects at

         ACM Trans. Multimedia Comput. Commun. Appl., Vol. 20, No. 2, Article 42. Publication date: September 2023.
42:6                                                                                                   M. Li et al.

different scales. In AlexNet [54], VGGNet [55], and other Backbone CNNs, the feature gradually
changes from coarse to fine with the deepening of the network, and the receptive field increases
with the increase of model depth so it already has the basic multi-scale representation ability.
   Further, for example, Inception [56] uses the parallel connection of multiple convolution ker-
nels of different sizes to enhance the scale representation ability of the model. Res2Net [57] uses
smaller filters and connects different filter groups in a hierarchical residual style, which can rep-
resent multi-scale features on a finer scale. HS-ResNet [58] uses the idea of similar order residual
connection to perform split and concatenation in multiple different paths, which constitutes the
multi-scale feature extraction of the block. In the above research, effective multi-scale feature ex-
traction methods are proposed in cascade, parallel, hierarchical, and other perspectives.
   For 1D-CNN deep learning based on pupil-wave data, this article combines the perspective of
cascade and parallel. While using convolution kernels of different sizes in parallel, convolution ker-
nels of each size are constructed respectively to reduce the size of convolution kernels in the cas-
cade direction. The combination of cascade and parallel multi-scale structure will help the model
to obtain more abundant multi-scale information.
   The attention mechanism is another essential part of the current deep learning structure, ini-
tially used in machine translation [59]. After much research, it has become an essential concept in
the field of deep learning and has a large number of applications in many fields, such as natural
language processing [60], computer vision [61–64], scene segmentation [65], and so on. The atten-
tion mechanism in the CNN model is mainly carried out in the space and channel dimensions of
the feature: SENet [61] uses global average pooling to squeeze the convolution feature map to get
the global features of the channel domain, and then excitation of the global features to learn the
relationship between the channels. ECA-Net [62] uses one-dimensional convolution instead of the
total connection layer based on SENet to obtain a more efficient channel domain attention model.
CBAM [63] aggregates channel dimension features using max pooling and average pooling, cal-
culates spatial attention using 2D convolution, and then combines it with channel attention. The
above attention methods all use the pooling method to locally aggregate features and generate
the attention matrix according to the feature intensity of each feature point, which is missing for
the calculation of long-distance dependence between feature points. However, many studies have
shown that people with higher depression risk have stronger ruminant thinking tendency [33, 34],
which is shown in that the subjects repeatedly reflect similar emotional experiences in the process
of stimulation. The self-attention method can effectively capture long-distance similarity [64, 65].
Therefore, to effectively capture these emotional representations with high similarity, we design
a width-channel attention module based on the self-attention method, which calculates the simi-
larity between feature points in the two dimensions of width and channel. Higher weight is given
to the areas with more substantial similarity and more distinct characteristics, and the model’s
performance of depression and anxiety level assessment is further improved.

parallel multi-scale convolution residual module and the width-channel attention module.

3.2   Cascade Parallel Multi-scale Convolution Residual Block
In assessing the level of depression and anxiety, the subjects in different states of emotional dam-
age had significant differences in emotional accumulation time, emotional response intensity, and
emotional duration during the process of human computer interaction when receiving virtual re-
ality emotional stimulation. Capturing pupil-wave features from different scales will be beneficial
for deep learning models to learn more multi-scale features related to depression and will Extract
and learn the features of depression more effectively. This article will explore the multi-scale im-
plementation of the model in convolutional neural networks’ cascade and parallel dimensions.
Figure 2 shows the fundamental backbone of the one-dimensional convolution neural network
proposed in this article: Cascade Parallel Multi-scale Convolution Residual Block (CP-MSC
ResBlock). The module is placed in the stage of each channel and used to extract multi-scale
features from the input of the stage.
    First of all, a 1 × 3 size convolution layer is used for spatial dimension fusion and channel
dimension abstract upgrading of the feature map entering the module; then, the Cascade Parallel
Multi-scale Convolution Layer, which is the core layer of this module, will extract multi-scale
features; finally, the 1 × 1 size convolution layer is used to align the dimensions of multi-scale
features and further increase the non-linear transformation of the network. At the same time, to
alleviate the gradient dispersion problem caused by the promotion of depth in the deep learning
network, we introduce residual connection to the module. The residual connection will further
introduce multi-scale information between layers for the module.
    The Cascade Parallel Multi-scale Convolution Layer consists of six channels: the three channels
on the left are parallel multi-scale channels with the size of 1 × 3, 1 × 5, and 1 × 7, and the three
channels on the proper use cascade multi-scale convolution are based on the three channels on
the left to extract more fine-grained visual field features. The success of Inception [56] shows that
it is more effective to use convolution kernels of different sizes for multi-scale feature extraction

layer by layer, and the receptive field can be further increased. The addition of cascade convo-

model.

3.3   Width-channel Attention Module
The ruminant thinking characteristic of people with high risk of depression will lead to similar
emotional representations in the process of stimulation [33, 34], especially the process of human
computer interaction in the virtual reality scenes with strong emotional experience. At the same
time, there is widespread information redundancy in time-series signals [36]. Therefore, giving
higher weight to the regions with richer representation of depression and anxiety, the feature
points with more substantial similarities between feature points will effectively improve the effi-
ciency and performance of the model in depression and anxiety level assessment. Figure 3 shows
the width-channel attention module, which aims to introduce the self-attention mechanism into
the task of assessing the level of depression, capture context dependencies in width and channel di-
mensions, and give higher weights to more context-sensitive data points. For the one-dimensional

3.4    Fully Connected Layers
After the dual-channel feature fusion layer, we connected two fully connected layers with de-
creasing number of neurons (128 neurons and 64 neurons, respectively) to product channel-fusion
evaluation. The fully connected layers with decreasing number of neurons can improve the fitting
ability of the model.
   At the same time, in order to test the evaluation ability of each single-channel model, two
fully connected layers (64 neurons and 32 neurons, respectively) are connected after each channel
model, which can be evaluated respectively. During the training, the two channel models will be
trained separately, then the weight of the convolution layers of the two channels will be fixed, and
only the fully connected layers of the fusion model will be fine-tuned. In order to prevent model
overfitting, we use L2 regularization and Dropout techniques at all fully connected layers.

stopped when the loss changes of the validation set in the epoch are less than 0.1. The initialization
parameters in the model are generated by the normal distribution.

5 CONCLUSION
Depression and anxiety have a significant impact on both society and individuals. The existing
depression assessment studies are mainly based on behavioral signals such as facial expression,
voice, language, and so on. At the same time, the research on anxiety assessment is missing. In this
study, we proposed a physiological signal of eye movement collected by human computer inter-
action of virtual reality emotional scenes, pupil-wave, which can directly represent the emotional

ACM Trans. Multimedia Comput. Commun. Appl., Vol. 20, No. 2, Article 42. Publication date: September 2023.
Automatic Assessment of Depression and Anxiety through Encoding Pupil-wave                                            42:19

state. Based on pupil-waves, a dual-channel one-dimensional convolution neural network using
a cascade parallel multi-scale convolution residual block and width-channel attention module is
proposed for depression level assessment and anxiety level assessment tasks. In the model, the
cascade parallel multi-scale convolution residual block introduces more multi-scale features to the
model through multiple cascade parallel convolution kernels with different sizes and shows signif-
icant advantages compared with other advanced multi-scale modules. Through the self-attention
mechanism, the width-channel attention module calculates the correlation between the feature
points in the two dimensions of the width of the one-dimensional signal and the channel and
gives higher weight to the regions with stronger similarity and more obvious features to improve
the feature learning ability of the model. Through the ablation experiment, we verify that the
attention module plays a significant role in improving the performance of the model in depres-
sion and anxiety level assessment tasks. At the same time, it also shows significant advantages
compared with other advanced attention modules. Based on this model, this article obtains the as-
sessment error of MAE = 3.05, RMSE = 4.11 in depression level assessment task and MAE = 1.85,
RMSE = 2.49 in anxiety level assessment task. By comparison, the error of depression assessment
task in this article is significantly better than the existing depression assessment methods. Since the
task of anxiety assessment is proposed for the first time in this article, this result is not compared
with other methods. Meanwhile, our proposed depression and anxiety level assessment method
can also be used in clinical depressive symptom assessment.
   One of the important factors leading to depression and anxiety is the stress from life, work,
and study, especially chronic stress. How to use the pupil-wave collected by human computer
interaction in virtual reality emotional scenes for stress assessment will become the next step
of work, especially how to use the heart rate specificity and multi-modal signals of pupil-wave
induced by virtual reality emotional scenes to assess depression, anxiety, and stress.
