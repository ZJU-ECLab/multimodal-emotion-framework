1.1 What are the emotions and research challenges?

Emotions are discussed by three factors in psychology:
Valence (Pleasure), Arousal and Dominance (Control) also
known as PAD [1, 9–11]. The first factor is Valence or
Pleasure, which shows the pleasure level, whether it is a
positive or a negative emotion. The second factor is
Arousal which talks about the amount of energy of emo-
tions. In literature, different amount of energy has been
named such as happiness, sadness and so on. And the third
factor is Dominance, which describes the strength level of
each emotion. It says how strong an emotion is.
   The last factor of Dominance (Control) is usually ignored
