Brief Discussion
Based on Study 1 and the emotional design model (Norman,
2004), Study 2 tested the effects of visual and behavioral levels of
emotional design on the multimedia learning of middle school
students. The results showed that behavioral emotional design
significantly induced positive emotions of learners (in partial
support of H4), which suggests that interacting with learning
environment can facilitate positive emotions in middle school
students. The findings provide evidence for the INTERACT
model demonstrating that interactive behavior in learning
environment can induce learners’ positive emotions (Moreno and
Mayer, 2007; Domagk et al., 2010). Especially when learners have
more control over the learning process, they may experience
more positive emotions. The results also revealed that learners
