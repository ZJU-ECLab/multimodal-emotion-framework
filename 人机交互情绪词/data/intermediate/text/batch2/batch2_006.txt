D. Application in a Virtual Scene
    We establish a virtual scene to testify the affective
model. In the scene, there are two virtual characters, the
one is ‘Robot’, which represents the user; the other is
‘Emma’, she can autonomously generate personalized
behavior according to the stimulus and can also express
affections through facial expressions and body
movements. They can interact with each other. We
designed a ‘homework’ event: Robot is responsible for
reminding Emma to do the homework, and each time she
reacts differently: from initial disgust, then anger, and
finally happy because Robot is going to take her to the
park and it express these different affections through her
body language as Figure 5 shows.
   Robot can produce different affective status according
to the external stimulation, it has autonomous behavior
ability. Of course, we also can endow it with different
personality or different stimulations, to verify the
affection model.
