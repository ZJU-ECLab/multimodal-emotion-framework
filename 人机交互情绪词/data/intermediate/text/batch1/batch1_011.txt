    ABSTRACT                                                                                                                                   KEYWORDS
    Gratitude is a moral emotion that demonstrates our appreciation of altruism. In psychology, feel-                                          Gratitude; conversational
    ing grateful is linked to an increase in well-being, yet there is a lack of HCI research on if gratitude                                   user interface; chatbot;
    can be cultivated through and with conversational agents. We quantitatively studied whether a                                              positive computing;
                                                                                                                                               emotions; well-being
    chatbot can increase people’s gratitude (N ¼ 133), as well as its influence on people’s positive and
    negative emotions. Compared to the control condition, a chatbot that shared gratitude interven-
    tions significantly enhanced people’s gratitude and positive emotions, while lowering negative
    emotions. Interestingly, people’s experience of gratitude differed from other positive emotions:
    Simple positive emotions, like joy, can go up while reported gratitude decreases. We share qualita-
    tive observations on how gratitude can be a complex emotional experience, encompassing
    positive and negative emotions, such as finding relief in admitting to a chatbot about one’s
    sadness over friendship during the COVID-19 pandemic.
